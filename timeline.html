How to read this map

01

The spine is singular

Every topic gets one primary home so the map stays navigable. Other relationships belong in cross-links, not duplicate branches.

02

Facets are lenses

Role, lifecycle stage, and maturity describe the primary learning context. They are useful filters, not ownership boundaries.

03

Concerns travel

Security, quality, reliability, governance, and operating practices recur from hardware through the human experience — the pillars behind the stack.

04

The MicroStrategy lens

Ten years inside one enterprise BI platform show what an analytics system must do. The lens marks what sat inside its server, what the customer stack beside it carried, and what has no counterpart — responsibilities, not feature-for-feature replacements.

“The useful question is not where a topic belongs. It is what the topic touches.”
