    How to read this map

    01

    The spine is singular

    Every topic gets one primary home so the map stays navigable. Other relationships belong in cross-links, not duplicate branches.

    02

    Facets are lenses

    Role, lifecycle stage, and maturity describe the primary learning context. They are useful filters, not ownership boundaries.

    03

    Concerns travel

    Security, quality, reliability, governance, and operating practices recur from hardware through the human experience.

    04

    The edges will grow

    The current cross-links are a reviewed seed set. The map becomes more valuable as prerequisites, alternatives, and enabling relationships are added.
